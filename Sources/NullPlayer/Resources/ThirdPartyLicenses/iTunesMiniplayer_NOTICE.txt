iTunes Miniplayer (Winamp Modern skin)

The default Winamp Modern skin, "iTunes Miniplayer.wal", is an unofficial,
fan-made skin by spacecowgoesmoo, inspired by the classic iTunes Miniplayer.
It is redistributed with NullPlayer with its author's permission:
https://github.com/ad-repo/nullplayer/issues/540

Source: https://github.com/spacecowgoesmoo/Old-iTunes-Miniplayer

The skin bundles the DejaVu Sans Regular and Bold fonts; see DejaVu_LICENSE.txt.
