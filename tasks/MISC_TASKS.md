| M34 | Two Left presses while a film is paused move it back 10 s, not 20 s | `VideoPlayerView.skipBackward` / `skipForward` compute the target from `mediaPlayer.time`, which VLC does not advance while paused until the seek lands, so a second press before that seeks from the same old time. Measured 2026-10-08 on the debug build (Cablemusic, Emby `Airplane!`): paused at 26.01 s, Left, Left 0.5 s apart, resume and pause read 16.63 s; a single Right from 15.38 s read 26.01 s. Every mode, free or parked. **Direction:** keep the last requested target while a seek is pending (or while paused) and step from it, falling back to `mediaPlayer.time` once playback reports a new time. **Done when** two Left presses while paused read about 20 s back on the next pause timestamp, and a single press while playing still moves 10 s. Skills: `video-playback`. |
| M35 | A Plex film replaced by another reports its stop with the new film's duration | `PlexVideoPlaybackReporter.reportState` captures `ratingKey`, position and type before its `Task`, but reads `currentDurationMs` (and `currentTitle` for the log line) inside it, after the `await`. When a film replaces a playing one, `endPreviousVideo` → `videoDidStop` → `reportState(.stopped)` is followed at once by `videoTrackDidStart` for the new one, which has reset both by the time the request is built. Measured 2026-10-08 on the debug build (Classic aquamp): `Airplane!` (1:27:42) at 111.8 s, then double-click `30 Rock S01E02` (21:22) logged `Reported state 'stopped' at 111766ms for 30 Rock - S01E02 - The Aftermath`: the key was Airplane!'s, the label and the `duration` sent were the episode's. `scrobble(ratingKey:)` reads the title late the same way. Jellyfin and Emby send only the id and position, so there only the `Reported stopped for` / `Reported playback start for` log labels are wrong. Same on the queued and the former row path. **Direction:** capture every value the request and its log line use before the `Task` in all three reporters, as `ratingKey` already is. **Done when** replacing a playing Plex film logs the stop under the old title and sends the old film's duration, and the Jellyfin and Emby labels name the old film. Agent-verifiable from the log; no on-screen signature. Skills: `video-playback` (*Server progress reporting*), `plex-integration`. |
