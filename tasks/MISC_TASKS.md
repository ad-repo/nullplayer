| M74 | A queue edit while a stream's gapless successor is queued, or a crossfade runs, marks the wrong track playing | Raised by CodeRabbit on PR #681 (2026-11-03); read from code, not reproduced. With gapless on, a stream queues its successor's URL in `scheduleNextTrackForGapless` and keeps only that row in `nextScheduledTrackIndex`; the end-of-track handoff in `trackDidFinish` makes `playlist[nextScheduledTrackIndex]` current. `moveTracks` (drag, skin-script moves) remaps that row since #681, but Sort, Reverse, Randomize (`reorderPlaylist`), `removeTrack` and the inserts (`insertTracksAfterCurrent`, `playNow`) leave it, and `playlistDidChange` only refreshes the *local* successor.
