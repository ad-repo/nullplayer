| M36 | A docked stack in a seamless-docking Original skin casts no shadow at all | Every Original view's `windowLayoutDidChange` calls `window.applyDockingShadow(isDocked:cornersChanged:)` (`Windows/Shared/NSWindow+SkinShadow.swift`), which sets `SkinWindowShadow.isSuppressed` on any window touching another while the skin's `seamlessDocking` > 0. Each window's shadow is shaped around that window alone, so leaving them on would draw a shadow along every seam, across the neighbour; hiding them keeps the seams invisible, but the whole stack then floats shadowless (NeonWave, EmeraldForge, SeaGlass and the other bundled seamless skins). Pre-dates the skin shadow: `main` did the same with `hasShadow` (PR #528). **Direction:** one shadow per connected group. Take the group from `WindowManager.findDockedWindows(to:)` (BFS over `groupMovableWindows`), pick one window deterministically (the main window when it is in the group) to cast the union of every member's rendered outline placed at its offset, and keep the rest suppressed. The knockout removes the whole union, so z-order among members does not matter. `SkinWindowShadow` frames itself and sizes the shape from `parent.frame`, so it needs a shape rect that can be larger than its parent's; the caster pulls again when membership or a member's relative frame changes (the next `windowLayoutDidChange`). **Done when** a docked main/EQ/playlist stack on NeonWave casts one shadow around its combined outline with nothing along the seams, pulling a window off gives both the stack and the window their own shadow, and a drag of the stack carries the shadow with no lag (`NP_SKIN_SHADOW_TRACE=1`, `winhelper capture-region`). Skills: `ui-guide` (*Window Drop Shadows*), `original-skin-guide`. |
